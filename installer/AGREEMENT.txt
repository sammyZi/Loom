3. PRIVACY - IT RUNS ON YOUR MACHINE

There is no Loom server, no account and no telemetry. Nothing about your use of
this app is reported to anyone.

What stays local:
  - Your chat history, in a SQLite database in %APPDATA%\ide-ai
  - Your settings, and your API keys, sealed with Windows DPAPI so they are
    readable only by your Windows account
  - The code in whatever folder you open

What leaves your machine: when you send a task, your prompt and the file
contents the agent needs are sent to the model provider you configured, using
your own API key. That provider's own privacy policy and retention terms govern
what happens to it from there, so it is worth reading them. Choosing a provider
is choosing whose terms apply.

The app makes no other network calls unless a task explicitly searches the web
or fetches a URL.


4. YOUR DATA IS YOURS

Uninstalling keeps your sessions and keys; the uninstaller only removes them if
you ask it to with -RemoveData. Deleting the folder above removes everything
the app has ever stored.
